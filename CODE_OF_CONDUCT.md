# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Builders a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Give context, accept feedback gracefully, and make space for newcomers.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, sustained
disruption, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without
permission are not acceptable. This applies in repository spaces and when a
person is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner, [@zikrulihsan](https://github.com/zikrulihsan),
using a private contact method listed on their profile. Do not open a public
issue containing personal or sensitive details.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith, protect the reporter's privacy
as far as practical, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Retaliation against a
person who reports a concern is itself a violation.
